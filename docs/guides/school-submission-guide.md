# Press Link — Guide for School Coordinators

**For:** the teacher or school head assigned to enter their school's Division
Schools Press Conference details.

**What you will do:** sign in, register your school paper, register the people
who will compete and the people who will coach them, enter each contest your
school is joining, then lock your submission when everything is final.

**Where:** <https://press-link-delta.vercel.app>

You do not need to finish in one sitting. Everything you save stays saved, and
you can sign out and come back until you lock your submission.

---

## Before you start

Have these in front of you:

- Your school's **DepEd School ID number** — this is your password.
- Your **school paper's** name, adviser, principal, and section/assistant heads,
  for English, Filipino, or both.
- The **complete names and sexes** of every learner competing.
- The **complete names and sexes** of every coach.
- The **list of contests** your school is joining.

---

## The order matters

The site unlocks each stage as you finish the one before it. This is deliberate
— an entry cannot name a participant who has not been registered yet.

```
Sign in
   ↓
1. School paper details          ← nothing else opens until this is saved
   ↓
2. Contest question              ← one question, either answer moves you on
   ↓
3. Participants and coaches      ← the people who will compete and coach
   ↓
4. Entries                       ← the contests, choosing from step 3
   ↓
5. Lock submission               ← final; only the division office can undo it
```

---

## Step 1 — Sign in

1. Go to <https://press-link-delta.vercel.app>.
2. Choose your **District**.
3. Choose your **School**. You can type to search the list.
4. In **School ID**, enter your school's DepEd School ID number.
5. Click **Sign in**.

Use the eye icon at the right of the field if you want to check what you typed.

**If sign-in fails**, confirm you picked the right school and that the ID number
has no spaces. If it still refuses, contact the division office — do not create a
second account.

---

## Step 2 — School paper details

As soon as you sign in the first time, the **School Paper** form opens by itself
and cannot be closed. This is intentional: it is the first thing the division
office needs.

There are two tabs, **English** and **Filipino**.

- Fill in **one or both**. One is enough to continue.
- Every box starts filled with **N/A**. If something does not apply to your
  school, leave it as N/A.
- Each language needs: the paper's name, the adviser and their sex, the
  principal, and **at least two** section or assistant heads.
- Use **Add** to include more than two heads.
- Click **Save English paper** (or Filipino) for each tab you filled in.

A tab shows **Complete** once it has been saved.

> **You only publish in one language?** Fill that tab, save it, and leave the
> other as N/A. You will not be asked for it again.

---

## Step 3 — The contest question

Once at least one language is saved, one question appears:

> **Are you submitting this school paper to the school paper contest?**

- **Yes, submit to the contest** — your paper details are entered in the school
  paper contest.
- **No, just save our information** — your details stay on record with the
  division office, and you are not entered in that particular contest.

**Both answers are fine, and both let you continue.** Answering No does *not*
stop you joining any other contest, and it does not sign you out.

You can change this answer later from the dashboard — **Change contest answer** —
right up until you lock your submission.

---

## Step 4 — Register your participants

Open the **Participants** tab under Roster.

For each learner:

1. **First name**, **Middle name** (leave blank if none), **Last name**.
2. Choose **Male** or **Female**.
3. Click **Add participant**.

Each learner is given a **division-wide number** (0001, 0002, …) automatically.
That number is theirs for the whole competition — you do not choose it and it is
never reused.

Names appear as **surname first** — "Dela Cruz, Ana Mercado" — so the division
office can sort them consistently.

### Register everyone once

Add a learner **once**, even if they are competing in more than one contest. You
will pick them from this list when you create entries.

### Limits per learner

| | Most a learner may join |
|---|---|
| Individual contests | **2** |
| Group contests | **1** |

When a learner has reached a limit, they still appear in the entry list but
cannot be selected, with the reason shown beside their name.

---

## Step 5 — Register your coaches

Open the **Coaches** tab, beside Participants.

For each coach:

1. **First name**, **Middle name** (leave blank if none), **Last name**.
2. Choose **Male** or **Female**.
3. Click **Add coach**.

Coaches are registered exactly like participants and display the same way.
Register each coach once, however many contests they handle.

---

## Step 6 — Enter your contests

With at least one participant and one coach registered, **Create Entry** becomes
available.

The wizard narrows the choice one step at a time:

1. **Category** — Individual or Group.
2. **Level** — Elementary or Secondary.
3. **Language** — English or Filipino.
4. **Event** — the specific contest.
5. **Participants and coaches** — who competes, and who coaches them.

Then **Save entry**.

### Who coaches whom

**Individual contests** ask for a coach under each contestant. Every contestant
needs one and takes exactly one, and adding a contestant opens the coach field
that belongs to it. The same coach may be chosen for two or three contestants on
one entry — that is normal, and you do not register them twice to do it.

**Group contests** ask for the coaches as a set, up to two, because a team
shares its coaches rather than splitting them between members.

### How many people per entry

| | Participants | Coaches |
|---|---|---|
| Individual contests | 1–3 | one for each participant |
| Most group contests | exactly 7 | 1–2 |
| Online Publishing | 2 or more | 1–2 |

The wizard tells you if a count is wrong before it will save.

### One entry per contest

Your school may have **one entry per contest**. Elementary English News Writing
and Secondary English News Writing are different contests, so both are allowed —
but you cannot create Elementary English News Writing twice.

### Changing an entry

Use the **⋯** menu on any entry row to **View**, **Edit** or **Delete** it, until
you lock your submission. View is a quick read of who is on the entry; Edit
reopens the wizard.

Entries you filed earlier already have a coach under each contestant. Those
entries named their coaches without recording which contestant each one was for,
so the matching was filled in for you rather than asking you to re-file every
entry. It may not be the pairing you intended — check it, and change what is
wrong.

---

## Step 7 — Lock your submission

The **Lock Submission** button sits at the top of the dashboard, beside School
Paper. It stays greyed out until you have created **at least one entry** — hover
over it to see what is still missing.

When you click it, a confirmation lists exactly what gets frozen:

- your school paper details and contest answer
- your participants and coaches
- every entry you have created

Confirm, and your whole submission becomes **read-only**.

### What locking means

- You can still **see everything** — nothing disappears.
- You can change **nothing**: no edits, no additions, no deletions.
- The button changes to **Submission Locked**, and a banner explains the state.
- **Only the division office can reopen it.**

> **Lock only when you are certain.** There is no undo on your side.

---

## If you need something changed after locking

Contact the division office and ask them to reopen your submission. An
administrator can unlock it from their School Papers page.

When they do:

- Everything becomes editable again.
- **Your contest answer is kept** — you are not asked the school paper question
  again.
- You lock it again yourself when the correction is done.

There is a separate administrator action that *does* reset your contest answer,
used when a school answered the question by mistake. If your answer is cleared,
you will simply be asked the question again on your next visit.

---

## Messages you may see

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Your submission is locked. Ask the division office to reopen it." | You tried to change something after locking. | Contact the division office. |
| "Create at least one entry before locking your submission." | The Lock button is not available yet. | Create at least one entry first. |
| "Your school already has an entry for this event." | That exact contest is already entered. | Edit the existing entry instead. |
| "This event requires at least N participants" | Too few learners ticked. | Add more, or pick a different contest. |
| "At least 1 coach is required" | No coach chosen. | Choose at least one coach. |
| "Choose a coach for every contestant" | An individual entry has a contestant with an empty coach field. | Fill in the coach under each contestant. |
| "This entry allows at most 2 coaches" | A group entry names three or more. | Remove the extra ones. |
| "The same coach cannot be added twice" | A group entry lists one coach in two rows. | Remove the duplicate. |
| "Already in 2 individual events" / "Already in a group event" | That learner has reached their limit. | Choose a different learner. |
| "Remove this participant from their entries first." | You tried to delete someone still in an entry. | Remove them from the entry, then delete. |
| "Save your school paper information first" | Stage 1 is not finished. | Save at least one language tab. |

## Quick checklist

Before you lock:

- [ ] School paper saved for every language your school publishes in
- [ ] Contest question answered
- [ ] Every competing learner registered, spelled as it should appear on a certificate
- [ ] Every coach registered
- [ ] One entry created for each contest your school is joining
- [ ] Each entry has the right learners, and the right coach under each of them
- [ ] Names checked — after locking, only the division office can correct them

---

## Common questions

**Can two people from my school work on this at once?**
There is one account per school, so avoid working in two places at the same
time — the second person's changes can overwrite the first's.

**Can I change a learner's name after adding them?**
Delete them and add them again, as long as they are not in an entry yet. If they
are, remove them from the entry first. After locking, ask the division office.

**Do I have to enter the school paper contest to join other contests?**
No. Answer **No, just save our information** and carry on entering any contest
you like.

**I answered the contest question wrongly.**
Use **Change contest answer** on the dashboard, any time before you lock.

**Do I need to finish in one sitting?**
No. Everything saves as you go. Sign out and return whenever you like.

**What if a learner should be in three individual contests?**
The limit is two individual contests and one group contest per learner. Contact
the division office if you believe an exception applies.
